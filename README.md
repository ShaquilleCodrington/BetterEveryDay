# BetterEveryDay

**Make becoming better every day feel easier than it did yesterday.**

BetterEveryDay is a free, open-source productivity ecosystem designed to help students and young professionals organize their work, track their progress, pursue their goals, and keep everything moving forward.

Built around the idea that becoming better shouldn't feel like another responsibility to manage, BetterEveryDay brings together the tools, structure, and visibility needed to turn intentions into action—and action into meaningful progress.

### Current Beta

- Windows desktop application
- Downloadable Windows executable installer
- Automatic application updates
- Offline-first experience
- Free during beta
- Active development and ongoing improvements

To install the desktop application, visit the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ShaquilleCodrington/BetterEveryDay/releases) page and download the latest Windows installer.

Once installed, the application will notify you when new updates become available.

### Windows Installation Notice

Windows may display a Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warning when installing BetterEveryDay because the installer may not yet have an established signing reputation.

If you downloaded the installer from the official [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ShaquilleCodrington/BetterEveryDay/releases) page, select **More info**, then **Run anyway**, if prompted.

## Our Mission

BetterEveryDay exists to reduce the friction between ideas and action.

Whether you're planning your day, organizing responsibilities, capturing ideas, developing habits, or working toward long-term goals, your productivity environment should make it easier to continue—not harder to manage.

Our goal is to build an ecosystem that grows alongside its users, bringing together productivity, continuity, and thoughtful collaboration without sacrificing the simplicity of the experience.

## Current Features

BetterEveryDay brings multiple productivity tools together in one connected workspace.

### Organize

- **Dashboard:** A central view of your workspace, responsibilities, activity, and progress.
- **Notebooks:** Capture and organize thoughts, notes, and ideas using flexible blocks.
- **Tasks:** Manage responsibilities, priorities, and connected work.
- **Journeys:** Organize and track progress toward personal goals.
- **Calendar:** Understand upcoming responsibilities and how your work fits into your schedule.
- **Folders:** Keep your workspace structured and organized.

### Focus

- **Congruence:** Dynamic visual environments that respond to your work and activity.
- **Sessions:** Structure focused work and track time spent.
- **Floating Timer:** Keep track of your focus sessions while working.
- **Activity Navigation:** Move between different areas of your workspace without losing your flow.

### Continuity

- **Offline-First:** Access and continue working within your workspace without depending on a constant internet connection.
- **Local and Cloud Continuity:** Maintain application state across supported environments through the storage layer.
- **Automatic Updates:** Receive notifications and updates as new desktop releases become available.
